Book a session, or ask Tawasol

Tawasol is the clinic’s virtual receptionist. She can find you an appointment with Dr. Bibi Alamiri, answer practical questions, and pass anything clinical straight to the doctor — in Arabic or English.

How booking works

Choose a time with Tawasol, pay through the secure link within 24 hours, and your appointment is confirmed — you then receive a short form and the clinic location on WhatsApp. There is no payment at the clinic.
