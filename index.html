Tawasol, The Guidebook

“Tawasol” is the Arabic word for connectedness. The guidebook brings together three methods of personal and interpersonal connectedness — the Tawasol Model, the Self-value Tree and the Boundaries Castle — for parents, adolescents and adults. Written by Dr. Bibi Alamiri with Sulaiman Boodai; illustrations by Marah Alkazak.

The book is sold on Amazon, not here. Nothing on this site asks you to buy it.
